# Credential Access

## Overview

Credential Access (TA0006) is a MITRE ATT&CK tactic that describes adversary activities focused on obtaining legitimate authentication material, including passwords, account credentials, authentication tokens, and other sensitive identity information.

Adversaries may attempt to obtain credentials through phishing, credential dumping, password attacks, browser data theft, authentication token abuse, and exploitation of identity management weaknesses.

Compromised credentials can provide unauthorized access to enterprise systems, cloud environments, privileged accounts, and internal network resources.

Credential access is a critical stage in many attack campaigns because stolen authentication material can enable privilege escalation, persistence, lateral movement, and unauthorized access without requiring the exploitation of additional vulnerabilities.

From a defensive security perspective, monitoring authentication events, privileged account activity, credential store access, suspicious process behavior, and identity provider logs is essential for identifying credential-related attacks.

This tactic is particularly relevant to Identity and Access Management (IAM), Privileged Access Management (PAM), endpoint security, and identity threat detection.
