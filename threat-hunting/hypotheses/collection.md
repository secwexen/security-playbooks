# Collection

## Overview

Collection is a MITRE ATT&CK tactic (TA0009) that describes adversary activities focused on gathering information, sensitive files, documents, and other valuable data from compromised environments.

Adversaries may collect information from endpoints, servers, databases, cloud platforms, email systems, network shares, and enterprise applications. Collected information may include confidential business documents, intellectual property, authentication-related data, customer records, and internal communications.

Collection activities often occur before data exfiltration, although adversaries may also collect information for reconnaissance, intelligence gathering, or operational planning.

From a defensive security perspective, identifying unauthorized data access, abnormal file operations, unusual database queries, and suspicious data aggregation is essential for detecting potential collection activity.

This tactic is particularly relevant to SOC operations, Data Loss Prevention (DLP), endpoint monitoring, cloud security, and threat hunting.
