# Ethics & Responsible Use Guidelines

This document defines the ethical principles and responsible-use requirements for Security Playbooks. It applies to users, contributors, researchers, and maintainers.

## 1. Educational Use Only

- Scripts, detection rules, labs, validation workflows, and attack scenarios are intended for education, defensive security, authorized research, and controlled lab use.
- Do not deploy or execute repository content in production or against third-party systems without explicit authorization.

## 2. Respect Privacy

- Do not access, collect, modify, disclose, or exfiltrate data, systems, or credentials without explicit permission.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, data-protection requirements, and organizational policies.

## 3. Legal Compliance

- Use of this repository must comply with applicable laws, regulations, contracts, and internal policies.
- The maintainers and contributors do not endorse or support illegal, harmful, or unauthorized activity.

## 4. Responsible Disclosure

- Report vulnerabilities, unsafe examples, exposed secrets, or other security issues responsibly through the project's designated reporting channel.
- Do not exploit or publicly disclose a vulnerability in a way that creates avoidable risk.

## 5. Collaboration Ethics

- Respect contributors and maintainers.
- Give appropriate credit when using repository content in research, education, or portfolios.
- Do not misrepresent the origin or status of project content.

## 6. Safe Environment

- Run scripts, scenarios, and validation activities only in controlled and authorized environments.
- Avoid actions that could affect real-world networks, users, services, or data.
- Preserve evidence and use the least disruptive approach appropriate to the objective.

## 7. Authorization and Scope

- Perform security testing only on systems, accounts, networks, applications, and data for which you have explicit authorization.
- Define the scope, rules of engagement, affected assets, and stop conditions before testing.
- Do not access, modify, disrupt, or extract data outside the authorized scope.
- Stop testing immediately if activity affects unintended or unauthorized systems.

## 8. Sensitive Data and Credential Handling

- Do not use real passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys, personal data, or confidential information in labs, examples, or test datasets, issues, or contributions.
- Use synthetic, test, or anonymized data whenever possible.
- Never publish credentials, personal data, or confidential information in issues, logs, datasets, or contributions.
- Remove and revoke any sensitive information that is accidentally exposed, then notify the appropriate owner.

## 9. No Authorization by Inclusion

- The inclusion of offensive techniques, attack scenarios, proof-of-concept material, detection-bypass examples, or adversary-emulation procedures does not authorize their use against any system, person, organization, or environment.
- This repository does not authorize unauthorized access, testing, disruption, data collection, or compromise.
- When authorization is unclear, do not proceed until permission is confirmed.

## 10. User Responsibility

- Users are responsible for evaluating the safety, legality, and potential impact of their actions.
- All repository content must be used for a legitimate defensive, educational, research, or explicitly authorized purpose.
